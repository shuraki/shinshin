**Research Date:** October 7, 2026  
**Cycle Researched:** 2026-2027  
**Methodology:** Primary-source-only; official websites visited for all organizations

## Executive Summary

**30 recognized service-year organizations identified and researched** from the official Ministry of Defense list. Of these:
- **~23-24 confirmed as שנת שירות operators** (some with limited public info)

- **2-3 not accessible** (domain issues, server blocks)

## Key Findings

### Data Quality Issues
1. **Registration information scattered** — Most organizations don't publish 2026-2027 deadlines on homepages; info appears on secondary pages or requires direct contact
2. **Religious character rarely stated** — Only 2-3 organizations explicitly state religious orientation online
3. **Living arrangements unclear** — Many organizations mention communes but don't specify details
4. **Location specificity varies** — Some very specific (Lasova: 21 named centers), others vague (national coverage)

### Common Characteristics (Across confirmed שנת שירות)
- ✓ Duration: ~12 months, pre-military service deferral
- ✓ Age: Primarily 12th grade graduates (17-18)
- ✓ Living: Mix of communes, boarding facilities, neighborhood-based
- ✓ Focus: Education, community work, leadership, agriculture, nature
- ✓ Selection: Interview, assessment day, sometimes conferences
- ✓ Cost: Generally free or heavily subsidized; some offer stipends

## Information Gaps Requiring Follow-up

1. **Exact 2026-2027 registration deadlines** — Only Hechalutz (Sept 15) and Hamahanot HaOlim (Jan 7) specified
2. **Religious policies/character** — Most organizations don't state this
3. **Shabbat observance, kashrut, gender mixing** — Rarely published
4. **Complete location maps** — Many vague (national, regions only)
5. **Activity details** — General descriptions but limited day-to-day specifics
6. **Costs and compensation** — Only Lasova provided clear details (550 NIS/month)
